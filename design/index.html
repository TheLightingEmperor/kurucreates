N° 02 · 3D PrintInteractive 3D

Web-Shooter Gauntlet

An Arcane-style wearable built procedurally in Blender. It comes as 49 watertight, 3D-printable STLs with a working spring-piston and palm trigger. Every part is checked for interference across the whole firing animation, and the full rebuild takes 2.4 seconds.

  • Blender · Python
  • Manifold booleans
  • Finger IK
  • STL + BOM

N° 03 · 3DInteractive 3D

Flintlock, Dismantled

Rebuilt from a single photo of an antique holster pistol: 40 separate parts, walnut and engraving textures generated in code, and a rig that cocks the hammer, opens the frizzen, pulls the trigger and takes the whole thing apart. It rebuilds headless in about 3 seconds.

  • Blender · Python
  • Drivers + rig
  • GLB / FBX / OBJ
