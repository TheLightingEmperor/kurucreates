Wasteland Scrambler

A post-apocalyptic scrambler built entirely from Python in Blender, so every part can be re-proportioned, re-detailed or rebuilt. The mechanics are engineered, not just styled: tyres, suspension and steering move through their full range without touching anything, and every cable is routed to a real component. An automated checker sweeps the rig and proves it.

Concept: the "wasteland scrambler" design by @nora.pragmatik, re-styled with its own livery and an original K hex badge on the tank.

Petrol teal Satin black Safety orange
