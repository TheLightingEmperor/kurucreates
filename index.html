About

Producer first. Builder second.

I started in production: scripts, shoots, edits and brand films for founders, realtors and creators. That work taught me how an idea moves from a vague feeling to a finished thing, and how much of the job is the boring machinery in between.

So I started building the machinery. Agents that run every morning, voice assistants that answer in English or Hindi, dashboards a whole team works from. I build with AI harnesses like Claude and Codex, which lets one person ship in days what used to need a dev team.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to be technical?

No. You tell me what eats your week, I build the thing, and you get a short walkthrough video plus written notes. If you can use email, you can run it.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It runs on your accounts, and you get the code and documentation. If we stop working together, everything keeps running.

What does it cost to run after it's built?

Usually $20–150 a month in usage, such as voice minutes and AI calls, paid directly to the providers on your accounts. I'll estimate it before we start.

Is the free demo really free?

Yes. It's built from your public website so you can judge the work before anything else. There's no obligation to buy anything.

Do you work white-label with agencies?

Yes. I build under your brand at partner rates, deliver to you, and never contact your clients directly.

Do you still take production work?

Yes. Brand films, AI UGC and avatar ads, short-form and post-production. The production portfolio has the reels.
